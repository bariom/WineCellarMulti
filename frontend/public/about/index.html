About

Who builds Vinaris

Vinaris is a private digital wine cellar for collectors, built in Ticino, Switzerland, by Omar Bariffi.

Who is behind Vinaris?

Vinaris is built by Omar Bariffi, a wine collector in Ticino and the founder of Vinaris. Work on the app started in 2026.

Why does Vinaris exist?

«I built Vinaris because spreadsheets and generic apps were no longer enough to follow purchases, waiting periods, drinking windows, and the memory of my collection. I now use it every day and keep designing it for cellars ranging from 20 to more than 1,000 bottles.» (Omar Bariffi)

Where is Vinaris based?

Vinaris is based in Ticino, Switzerland. Prices are in Swiss francs and the app is available in Italian and English.

What does Vinaris do?

Vinaris keeps track of the cellar you own: what you have and where it is, when to open each bottle and what it is worth today.

  • Bottles, storage locations, quantities and purchase price
  • A drinking window for every bottle, split into stages
  • Value estimates from wine-shop prices, with the sources linked
  • An AI sommelier that suggests what to open from your own cellar
  • Free up to 15 active labels; Reserve at CHF 6 a month

Where do the value estimates come from?

When you ask for an estimate, Vinaris searches the web for the bottle’s current prices at wine shops in the market you choose, for example Switzerland, and shows a link to each source. The value updates when you request a new estimate. The Sommelier runs on OpenAI models and only suggests bottles that are in your cellar.
