AI features (label recognition, receipt scanning, value estimates, the Sommelier) run on prepaid AI credit. You buy an AI Pack in the app, on any plan, and the cost of each AI operation is deducted from your balance. No external services to set up.

Pricing questions

What counts as an active label?

A wine you still have bottles of. Six bottles of the same Barolo 2016 are one label, and wines you have finished drinking no longer count.

Why is the annual plan cheaper?

CHF 60 a year instead of CHF 72 for twelve months: you save CHF 12, about 17%.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from the billing portal in the app. Payments go through Stripe.

Where do the value estimates come from?

When you ask for an estimate, Vinaris searches the web for current prices at wine shops in the market you choose, for example Switzerland, and links each source.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The demo opens a populated cellar without an account, and Tasting is free for up to 15 active labels.
