Features

What Vinaris does for your cellar

Vinaris is the digital cellar for the collector with a few hundred bottles: each one with its drinking window, its value and its story. On your computer or phone, with nothing to install.

Find

Every bottle with vintage, format, quantity and exact location. Even when there are hundreds.

Open at the right time

No more bottles forgotten at the back of the cellar: the drinking window tells you when to wait, when it is ideal and when not to put it off.

Follow the value

Price paid, current value and its trend over time, per wine and for the whole collection. Estimates start from current wine-shop prices in the market you choose, with a link to each source.

Remember

Notes, scores, occasions and photos of the evening stay even after the bottle is gone.

The essentials

The tools you expect, designed to work together and stay simple with a few hundred bottles. On Reserve there is no label limit, even past 1,000 bottles.

  • Complete inventory. Producer, vintage, format, quantity and status.
  • Locations and racks. Know where every bottle is and move it in a tap.
  • Drinking windows. Too young, ideal, drink soon.
  • Value and history. Market estimates and their trend over time.
  • Label recognition. Photograph the bottle and the record fills itself in.
  • Notes and scores. Your judgement, bottle after bottle.
  • Wishlist. Target prices and priorities before you buy.
  • Orders and deliveries. En primeur, collections and arrivals on a timeline.
  • Filters and statistics. By region, status, maturity, value, format.
  • Import and export. From a CellarTracker CSV or a Vinaris backup.
  • Several cellars. Home, second home, family cellar: one account.
  • Computer and phone. In your browser, nothing to install.

Beyond inventory

From invoice to cellar

Photograph the receipt. The bottles add themselves.

Take a photo of an invoice or receipt, or upload a PDF: Vinaris recognises wines, vintages, quantities and prices. You check and confirm.

  • Every purchase stays linked to its original document
  • Separate lots and prices for each purchase
  • Recognises the wines already in your cellar

Your cellar’s sommelier

Advice that starts from the wines you already own.

Describe the dish or the occasion. The Sommelier picks the bottles in your cellar that are at the right moment, and tells you when one deserves to be opened.

  • Pairings only with wines in your cellar
  • Takes maturity and preferences into account
  • At a restaurant, helps you choose from the list

One cellar, several owners

Shared bottles, finally clear.

A case bought by three, a family cellar, a purchase among friends: Vinaris keeps track of everyone’s share and which bottles belong to whom.

  • Percentage shares for every wine
  • Invitations with full or read-only access
  • Value and bottles calculated on your share

In the wine shop, with Vinaris Riserva

In front of the shelf, one photo is enough.

Photograph the wall of bottles: Vinaris reads the labels and tells you which one to buy for your taste, your budget and your cellar. It marks the best choices right on the photo.

  • First, second and third choice circled on the photo
  • Budget, type and occasion: you set the limits
  • Skips wines you already own, or fills the gaps in your cellar

Your taste, measured

Vinaris learns what you really like.

Your ratings build a personal sensory signature: body, acidity, tannins, aromas. And every wine shows how well it matches you.

  • A profile that sharpens with every bottle
  • The regions and grapes you love, highlighted
  • New discoveries that suit your palate

Memories, not just notes

The wine ends. The evening stays.

Add a photo to a tasting: people, place, occasion. Your toasts become an album to browse, on a table of polaroids or on a world map.

  • Every memory linked to the wine and its record
  • Search by month or occasion
  • Find your evenings on a map of places

For restaurants too

From private cellar to wine list.

The same Vinaris in restaurant mode: a public wine list, sales by the bottle and by the glass, margins and stock to reorder, in one place.

  • An always up-to-date wine list to share with a link
  • Revenue, costs and margins by period
  • Alerts on references running low

And more

  • Cellar strategy. A plan that balances drinking, ageing and capital.
  • Buying advice. Suggestions that suit your taste, including ones available near you.
  • Offers assessed. A wishlist price, compared with the market.
  • Your dashboard. Choose, move and resize the widgets that matter to you.
  • Wine Pulse. News from the wine world, with its source.
